Subject: Platform use case — reselling provisioned ComfyUI instances to our customers

Hi Vast team,

We're building a hosted ComfyUI product (similar in shape to RunComfy): our
customers click "rent", we provision a dedicated GPU instance on their behalf
with our preloaded ComfyUI environment, and we bill them hourly. Vast would be
the underlying compute — we'd be renting datacenter/verified offers via your
API, one instance per active customer session, with per-second billing on our
side mapped to your billing.

Before we commit engineering to this, we want written confirmation that this
use is fine under your Terms of Service — specifically the clauses around
"service bureau" use and competing services. To be clear about what we are and
aren't doing:

- We are NOT reselling raw Vast GPU access or building a GPU marketplace;
  customers never see Vast, choose hosts, or run arbitrary containers.
- Customers interact only with our managed ComfyUI application layer; we
  control the image, the models, and the network exposure.
- Expected initial scale: single-digit concurrent instances, growing with
  demand; primarily RTX 4090 / RTX 5090 / A6000-class datacenter offers.

1. Some of our image/video models are fine-tunes capable of legal adult

2. Is there a partner/volume program (reserved pricing, capacity guarantees,
