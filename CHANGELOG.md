## 2026-09-19 — The composer takes the whole width, and the studio works under a thumb

- Status: Uncommitted. Frontend only (`packages/open-generative-ai`). `dist/` WAS rebuilt (`npm run vite:build`), so the 8765 app serves this — any tab open at the time needs one reload, because a build renames the hashed chunks.
- Asked for (Liam, 2026-09-19): "during generations (or maybe also generally in the studio views), the chat composer is getting squished instead of taking up the full width. fix it. also overall improve the ui for mobile too. i want it to look mobile native on mobile devices."
- **The squish, root cause.** `StudioFrame` published the rail's width as an INLINE custom property (`--frame-rail-w: 96px`, Video 108), and `base.css` tried to zero it below sm. An inline custom property beats any stylesheet rule regardless of the media query around it, so that rule had never applied once — and `ComposerFloat` carried its own `right: ${railWidth}px` copy that no breakpoint could reach at all. The rail aside is `hidden sm:flex`, so on a phone four floating layers held 96px open for something `display:none`. Measured at 375px before: the composer panel **235px of 375**, and Generate — every child of the action row being `shrink-0` in a row that could not wrap — drawn **111px outside the panel it lives in**. After: **354px**, Generate inside it, 44px tall, no horizontal overflow. Desktop unchanged and measured: 880px panel, 102x35 press, 84px rail pill, `--frame-rail-w: 96px`.
- The fix is two property names, so the gate is pure CSS and needs no listener: the frame publishes `--frame-rail-px`; `base.css` is the only place `--frame-rail-w` is written (`0px`, then `var(--frame-rail-px)` from `min-width: 640px`, matching Tailwind's own `sm` so the band and the rail turn on at the same pixel). Same shape for `--frame-stage-gap` / `--frame-tabs-reserve` (desktop air the stage keeps, smaller on a phone), `--frame-rail-strip` and `--app-composer-h`. A [2026-09-11 note](Memory) claimed this was fixed then; `git log -S railHidden` finds nothing — it never landed.
- **Two house patterns** carry most of the rest. `touch:` (`@media (pointer: coarse)`) is the variant for hit targets — width says how much room there is, this says how precise the pointer is. It is a PLUGIN variant and must stay one: a `raw` screen in `theme.screens` cannot be inverted into a max-width and Tailwind then emits **no `max-*` variants at all**, silently (measured — `max-sm:hidden` compiled to nothing, and two agents wrote load-bearing comments asserting that was permanent). And `--ctl-xs/sm/md/lg` (24/28/36/44) rise to 32/34/44/48 under a coarse pointer, now mapped to BOTH `h-ctl-*` and `w-ctl-*` — the widths were hard-coded px, so the bump used to stretch every IconButton into a lozenge.
- App-wide: `font-size: 16px` on every field under a coarse pointer, at a specificity that beats one utility (a bare `textarea` selector loses to `text-[13.5px]`, which is the music studio's lyrics box — the one field people type paragraphs into); `future: { hoverOnlyWhenSupported: true }`, so ~520 `hover:` styles stop latching on whatever a finger touched last; `touch-action: manipulation` and a honey tap highlight; `overscroll-behavior: contain` + momentum on the three scroller classes — `.custom-scrollbar` was applied 19 times and **defined nowhere**; `Modal` as a real bottom sheet below sm (grab handle, `88dvh`, safe-area padding); `MenuItem` at `touch:py-3`, which makes every menu in the app a 44px surface, so "put it behind a menu" became a complete answer for a control with nowhere else to go.

- Shipped as **Fight**, a chip beside Style and UGC under **More** (H3 only). `src/lib/h3CombatPreset.js` holds the rules; `src/studios/video/CombatMenu.jsx` draws them. Arming moves four dials where the lane has them — size to **Max** (H3's trained ~1 MP canvas, where `resolutionsFor` stops), **Fast high-res** on to pay for it, **Spectrum** OFF, **Motion smoothing** 2x — selects an installed fight LoRA, and appends one `Fight direction:` sentence to the prompt.
- The WHY for each, against the write-up: "realism is improved via prompt and generation MP" → Max; "better quality at higher MP in less time" bought there with a distilled 4-step checkpoint we do not ship → our equivalent lever is the two-pass latent upscale, which also pairs with Max because below the registry's `min_upscale_factor` 1.3 the compiler drops back to single-pass; "fast motion is a limitation … frame interpolation helps a lot at almost no extra cost" → the `FrameInterpolate` node that has been in every H3 graph since the lane shipped and that nothing in the studio had ever turned on; "combat requires patience" → Spectrum off, whose own registry note says forecasting softens fine detail and blooms highlights, which is what a contact frame is made of.
- The restore contract is the whole reason it carries a snapshot. Arming records what each APPLICABLE dial held (a dial the lane does not have is never snapshotted, so disarming cannot write back a value that lane never carried), and turning it off replays exactly that and strips the direction sentence. A dial that was unset is recorded as `null`, not `undefined`, because JSON drops the latter and the dial would then never come back.
- The preset follows you between lanes and RE-ARMS when it does, over the restored setup rather than the armed one. Found live and fixed: without that, arming on the rented H3 lane, switching to the Apple-silicon one and switching back snapshotted Max/on/2x as "what it was before", and that lane could never be turned off again. A restored value the new lane does not offer is dropped in favour of the lane's own default.
- What it deliberately does NOT do, said in the panel instead: shorten your clip (H3 spreads the action over whatever timeline it is given — that is what makes a long fight read as slow motion), fix faces in wide shots (the write-up's FaceRefine node is not on these lanes and is single-subject anyway), or attach the expression / set-continuity references the write-up's best results came from. With no fight LoRA installed it says so and names the source rather than arming four of five things quietly.
- NEW SETTING, and the only wiring this needed: **Motion smoothing** in the Advanced drawer (`interpolate`), registry-gated on `accepts: interpolate` exactly like Spectrum and Fast high-res beside it, and carried studio → `/api/media-studio/video/start` → `start_video` → the MCP's `params` record (a new `_slot_params`, which now carries `steps` and `interpolate` together).
